1. THE VISION — WHY BUILD A 3D WEB RACER?

Most browser games today are either lightweight 2D hyper-casual titles or bloated web ports that take forever to download. I wanted to push the boundaries of what is possible on the modern open web: What if you could open a browser tab, share a 6-digit room code with your friends on Discord or WhatsApp, and immediately jump into an intense 60-FPS 3D racing brawl with no launcher, no login wall, and zero friction?

I combined two distinct gameplay obsessions that I love:

  • Trackmania’s micro-second obsession: Perfect apex lines, 1-lap Time Sprint trials, and global milliseconds leaderboards.
  • Smash Karts & Mario Kart’s laugh-out-loud party mayhem: Bumper ramming, slipstream drafting, arena sumo knockouts, and high-impact physical chaos.
🎯

The Single Universal Car Decision

Early on, I experimented with multiple car classes. But I quickly realized that the best competitive games — from Rocket League to Trackmania — succeed because everyone drives the exact same fair physics chassis. Winning comes down purely to drift control, boost timing, and line mastery. Customization should be 100% cosmetic (Paints, Shades, Trails)!

⚙️

2. THE ENGINEERING — WASM RAPIER3D PHYSICS

The hardest part of any 3D driving game is vehicle physics. Standard JavaScript physics engines often suffer from garbage collection (GC) micro-stutters, causing cars to glitch or fall through track floor colliders during high-speed loops.

To solve this, I compiled Rapier3D (written in Rust) directly into WebAssembly (WASM). Running physics inside WASM unlocked unbelievable benefits:

  • Zero Floor Clipping: Continuous collision detection (CCD) prevents cars from sinking or falling off bankings even at 150 km/h.
  • Authentic Drift Momentum: True tire lateral slip simulation that dynamically kicks the rear out during handbrake turns while allowing snappy counter-steering.
  • Air Launch & Recoil Trajectories: When you hit a kicker ramp or get rammed in Sky Rumble, realistic 3D torque vectors spin and tumble the car through the clouds.
🌐

3. ZERO-LAG MULTIPLAYER — WEBRTC MESH

Instead of hosting expensive, centralized game servers that add artificial ping delay, AirSkid uses a decentralized Peer-to-Peer (P2P) WebRTC mesh.

🚀

How P2P Room Sync Works

One player acts as Room Host, and peers connect directly over UDP data channels. We use client-side prediction, dead-reckoning extrapolation, and Hermite quaternion interpolation so opponent cars move buttery smooth at 60 FPS even across high-ping cellular networks!

🎨

4. THE BRUTOPIA ART STYLE & UI SCALE MATRIX

Tired of generic, dark, washed-out tech UI, I crafted the Brutopia Design System specifically for AirSkid:

  • Chunky Neo-Brutalist Lines: Bold 3.5px solid black borders and tactile 5px hard drop shadows that physically depress when clicked.
  • High-Energy Arcade Primaries: Golden Neo-Yellow (`#FFE600`), Electric Cyan (`#00D2FF`), and Coral Smash Red (`#FF385C`).
  • Universal Screen Matrix (`UIScaleEngine`): Whether you're playing on a 4K desktop monitor, an iPad, or a budget Android smartphone, the entire UI auto-scales dynamically with zero overlapping cards.

5. THE FULL TECHNOLOGY STACK

Here are the core technologies and engines powering AirSkid behind the scenes:

3D GRAPHICS 🎮

Three.js (WebGL)

Custom camera smoothing, procedural skybox clouds, real-time shadow cascades, and dynamic burnout smoke particles.

PHYSICS 🦀

Rapier3D WASM

Rust-compiled WebAssembly physics engine running at an unwavering 60Hz tick rate for authentic vehicle kinematics.

NETWORKING 🌐

WebRTC & PeerJS

Decentralized peer-to-peer data channels transmitting car transforms and bumper impulse packets at low latency.

LANGUAGE ⚡

TypeScript & Vite

Strictly-typed modular architecture built with Vite for lightning-fast HMR and minified sub-second builds.

BACKEND 🔥

Google Firebase

Firestore dual-track sprint leaderboards (Normal & Hardcore) and Google OAuth for persistent cloud garages.

AUDIO 🔊

Web Audio Synth

Procedural oscillator synthesis for engine rev pitch-shifting, drift tire screeches, and celebratory arcade jingles.
