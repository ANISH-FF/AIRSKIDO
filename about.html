THE SOLO BATTLE: FROM 1,000+ BUGS TO HYPERSPEED

Building a complete 3D racing game with real-time multiplayer completely alone was brutally difficult. There was no dev team, no publisher funding, and no commercial engine shortcuts—just one developer working through endless sleepless nights to create something extraordinary directly in a web browser.

Along the way, Anish encountered thousands of stubborn bugs: karts launching into outer space from invisible bumps, wheels glitching through track colliders, multiplayer car desyncs, and weird browser frame drops. Instead of giving up or bloating the game with heavy third-party engines, every single bug was tracked down, isolated, and crushed solo.

The result is the lightest (<5MB) and fastest 3D multiplayer racing game across the entire internet. It cold-boots in under 0.4 seconds on any browser or mobile device, locks at buttery-smooth 60 FPS, and lets you battle friends in real-time with zero installs or downloads.
