AIRSKIDO PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026 // EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY

At AIRSKIDO, operated by full-stack developer Anish Srivastava, we believe web gaming should be fast, transparent, and completely respectful of your privacy. We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize player data. This policy outlines exactly what information is collected, how it is secured, and your rights as a player.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only the minimal data strictly necessary to authenticate accounts, maintain garage progression, and facilitate multiplayer racing:

  • Player Account Authentication: When you sign in to your player account, we securely access your public User ID, Display Name, Profile Photo URL, and Email Address. We never access, handle, or store personal account passwords.
  • Garage & Gameplay State: Pilot level, XP progression, earned Coins, unlocked cosmetic items (Skins, Hats, Glasses), and track lap time records saved securely in encrypted cloud servers.
  • Transient Network Telemetry: Ephemeral connection handshakes strictly utilized by our real-time game web servers and WebSockets to synchronize multiplayer racing rooms. These are transient and are never permanently logged or stored.
  • Local Browser Storage: Client preferences such as audio volume levels, graphics performance toggles, and cached racer nicknames stored locally on your device via the localStorage API.

Advertising & Analytics Disclosure

To support free arcade access for all players, AIRSKIDO may partner with certified digital advertising networks (including Google AdSense) and analytics services to serve relevant ads and evaluate traffic under standard digital privacy frameworks.

2. HOW YOUR DATA IS USED

Your information is used exclusively for functional gameplay operations:

  • To save and synchronize your garage items, Coins, and cosmetic unlocks across browsers.
  • To verify lap times and display your pilot name on the official Global Top 100 Time Sprint leaderboards.
  • To connect with our secure real-time game web servers and WebSockets for lag-free multiplayer racing rooms.
  • To protect the game against unauthorized bot exploitation, packet injection, and physics tampering.

We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

3. SECURITY & STORAGE

AIRSKIDO’s database and profile infrastructure is hosted on enterprise-grade secure cloud servers. All data in transit is encrypted using 256-bit TLS/HTTPS protocols. Data stored at rest is protected with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

Server-side access rules strictly enforce that authenticated users can only modify their own individual garage profiles, preventing unauthorized access or data tampering.

4. TRADEMARK, IP & ANTI-EXPLOIT PROTECTION

AIRSKIDO™ is a registered and common-law proprietary trademark and intellectual property authored and owned exclusively by Anish Srivastava.

We strictly prohibit any unauthorized scraping of leaderboard data, automated harvesting of player records, reverse engineering of network endpoints, or exploiting client-server pipelines. Any attempt to inject malicious packets, exploit database rules, or replicate proprietary assets will trigger instant account termination, firewall blacklisting, and aggressive legal remedies under international intellectual property and cyber protection statutes.
