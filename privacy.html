AIRSKID RACING // PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to AirSkid! We believe that arcade web gaming should be fast, fun, and completely transparent. We do not track your web browsing, we do not sell personal data, and we do not require invasive accounts just to have fun with friends.

1. Core Philosophy 2. What We Collect 3. P2P Multiplayer 4. Google Cloud Sync 5. Local Storage 6. Fair Play & Safety 7. Contact Developer
🛡️

1. CORE PRIVACY PHILOSOPHY

AirSkid is an indie arcade browser racing and combat game built using modern web standards (WebGL, Three.js, WebAssembly Rapier3D, and WebRTC). Our architecture is designed from the ground up as offline-first and decentralized.

✨

No Forced Account Creation

You can hop in right away as a Guest Pilot. Your callsign, earned coins, equipped liveries, and best sprint lap times are saved immediately on your own device.

📊

2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT (AND WHAT WE DON'T)

We are uncompromisingly clear about what data touches the game:

  • In-Game Telemetry & Progression: Callsign name (e.g. "Speedster #AS-1204"), coins earned, level XP, unlocked liveries/glasses, and completed lap sprint times.
  • Global Sprint Leaderboard: When you finish a 1-lap record run, your callsign, ID code, and elapsed time are posted to the public Hall of Fame so other racers can challenge your record.
  • What We NEVER Collect: No real names, no credit card or financial numbers, no home addresses, no device IMEI or advertising identifiers, and zero third-party cross-site trackers.
🌐

3. PEER-TO-PEER (P2P) MULTIPLAYER MESH

AirSkid's multiplayer rooms run on a decentralized Peer-to-Peer WebRTC network mesh (powered by PeerJS).

When you join or host a multiplayer room:

  • Your browser connects directly to the room host and fellow racers.
  • Positions, rotations, velocities, and bumper hit impulses are sent in real-time between devices.
  • These network packets are strictly ephemeral — they are processed on the fly and discarded instantly. We do not store or replay your match movements on central servers.
☁️

4. OPTIONAL GOOGLE CLOUD ACCOUNT BACKUP

If you wish to synchronize your coins, unlocked cosmetics, and VIP status across multiple devices, you can optionally choose to link your Google account via Firebase Authentication.

🔒

Google Firebase Security

Authentication is handled securely through Google's OAuth protocol. AirSkid never sees or stores your Google password. We only read your public user ID to associate your saved garage data.

💾

5. LOCAL STORAGE & ZERO AD TRACKING

AirSkid uses your browser's standard localStorage API solely to store game preferences:

  • Master audio volume, sound effects, and engine rev toggles.
  • Graphics rendering resolution scale (720p / 1080p).
  • Your equipped car paint livery, glasses, and particle trails.
  • Offline cached personal best records for Normal and Hardcore tracks.

We do not inject third-party ad network tracking pixels, behavioral retargeting cookies, or fingerprinting scripts.

👶

6. PLAYER SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

AirSkid is designed for gamers of all ages. In accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards:

  • We do not knowingly solicit personal contact information from anyone.
  • Callsigns are limited to 11 characters to prevent posting personal data.
  • All in-game purchases use virtual coins and gems earned purely through gameplay feats or optional rewards.
